Visa-free regime applies only to short-term non-profit stays .
If the purpose of stay is employment, Schengen visa for the purpose of employment  or Schengen visa for the purpose of seasonal employment is required.
1) States whose citizens are exempt from the visa requirement for travel to the  Schengen Area :
BOSNIA AND HERZEGOVINA*
SAINT KITTS AND NEVIS
SAINT VINCENT AND THE GRENADINES
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA
* Citizens of Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Moldova, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Serbia and Ukraine can travel visa-free only with biometric passports .
**  Visa-free regime applies to holders of passports issued by Taiwan only if their passport contains an identity card number.
2) Territorial and autonomous units which are not recognized as states by at least one EU Member State and whose residents are exempt from the visa requirement for travel to the  Schengen Area :
KOSOVO as defined by the UNSCR 1244 of June 10, 1999 –  for holders of biometric passports only
3) Special administrative regions of the People's Republic of China whose residents are exempt from the visa requirement for travel to the  Schengen Area :
HONG KONG –  for holders of a  "Hong Kong Special Administrative Region"  passport  only
MACAO –  holders of a  "Região Administrativa Especial de Macau"  passport  only
4) British citizens who are not nationals of the United Kingdom (British Nationals (Overseas) and whose residents are exempt from the visa requirement for travel to the  Schengen Area :
British Nationals (Overseas)
British Overseas Territories Citizens
