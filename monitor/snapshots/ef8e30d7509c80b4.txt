The prepaid visa options
Allows for one entry into Zimbabwe within a specified period of one (1) month, requiring a new visa for subsequent entries.
Permits two separate entries into Zimbabwe within a designated timeframe of three (3) months, ideal for travelers with multiple visits planned.
Grants the holder multiple entries into Zimbabwe over a specified period of six (6) months, facilitating frequent travel for business or tourism purposes.

The paid on arrival visa options
Allows eligible travelers to pay for and obtain a visa upon arrival at the destination's port of entry, simplifying the entry process for short-term visits.
This visa is valid for a specified period of one (1) month.
All declarations can be pre-filled online.
Double Entry Visa On Arrival
Enables eligible travelers to pay for and obtain a visa allowing two entries upon arrival at the destination's port of entry, providing flexibility for multiple visits.
This visa is valid within a designated timeframe of three (3) months.
All declarations can be pre-filled online.
Multiple Entry Visa On Arrival
Allows eligible travelers to pay for and obtain a visa allowing multiple entries upon arrival at the destination's port of entry, catering for frequent visitors.
This visa is valid within a designated timeframe of six (6) months.
All declarations can be pre-filled online.
(This option is only available for the UK, USA and Canada)
KAZA UNIVISA On Arrival
A variant of the KAZA Univisa paid for and obtained upon arrival at the destination's port of entry, offering convenience for travelers exploring multiple countries within the KAZA TFCA.
This visa is valid for a specified period of one (1) month.
All declarations can be pre-filled online.
The free entry declaration
Zimbabwean citizens, as well as travelers from select countries, can enter without obtaining a visa beforehand.
Any declaration required before arrival can be pre-filled online.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
An e-Visa is an online visa application platform that allows foreign nationals to apply for their Zimbabwe Visa.
It provides a convenient alternative to the traditional landing visa, where payment can be made upon arrival.
However, Zimbabwean officials retain the right to deny entry without explanation.
How long does it take to obtain an eVisa?
On average, the processing time for an e-Visa is seven (7) working days.
Please note that this duration may vary due to various factors.
What are the advantages of an eVisa?
The e-Visa offers the convenience of global application, saving time compared to traditional methods at Zimbabwean missions or entry ports.
Who can apply for an eVisa?
Check your eligibility by reviewing the categories at the end of this page.
What types of visas are available?
Single Entry : Allows for a single entry with no re-entry without obtaining a new visa.
Double Entry : Permits two entries within three months from the date of issue.
Multiple Entry : Permits any number of entries within six months from the date of issue.
KAZA UNIVISA : A visa valid for multipe countries.
Visa On Arrival : A quick, flexible travel document obtained upon arrival, with broad eligibility and paid on arrival.
Check specific country requirements in Visa Regimes .
Do I need to enter Zimbabwe on the specified date in my application?
The validity period begins from the specified date, allowing entry on any date within this period.
How is my information protected during the eVisa application process?
The Department of Immigration of Zimbabwe ensures data confidentiality.
Information and eVisa details are stored in high-security systems.
The applicant is responsible for the safety of both soft and hard copies, with a QR code storing encrypted information.
Can I apply for an eVisa amendment if my travel dates change?
No, a new e-Visa must be obtained.
What if my eVisa information doesn't match my travel document?
Your eVisa is invalid.
Contact the Immigration Office for amendments.
Is a refund available for unused eVisas?
No, refunds are not issued for unused eVisas.
What if I need to correct information in my eVisa application?
Create a new visa application or contact the Zimbabwe Immigration Department for assistance.
When will I receive my eVisa after completing the application?
You will receive an email confirming the successful submission of your eVisa application form.
Further notices will be sent via email.
Will I get a refund if my eVisa application is denied?
No, the payment is for processing and does not guarantee approval.
How long before my travel date should I apply for an eVisa?
Apply at least 3 weeks before your travel date.
How long is my eVisa valid for?
The e-Visa is valid for one (1) month for a single entry or a KAZA UNIVISA, three (3) months for a double entry and six (6) months for a multiple entry.
Starting from the date of issue.
Which browsers are compatible with this application?
Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Mozilla Firefox are highly recommended browsers.
