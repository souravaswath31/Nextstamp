Diplomatic relations The United Kingdom recognized Montenegro's independence and diplomatic relations were established between the two countries on 13 June 2006.
Until the appointment of the ambassador, the Embassy of Montenegro in the United Kingdom currently operates at the level of Chargé d'Affaires a.i.
The Ambassador of the United Kingdom to Montenegro is Dawn McKen .
