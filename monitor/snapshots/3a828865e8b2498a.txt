Some Norwegian embassies are responsible for several countries.
One embassy may process visa applications from more than one country.
Your country is one of them.
In some cases, Norway is represented by another country for visa applications.
This means you might need to apply through an embassy from another country.
To get the correct information or start your visa application, please contact the regional embassy or the embassy of another country that handles visas for Norway in your country.
You can find the contact information and a link to their website below.
A visitor visa allows you to stay in Norway and other Schengen countries for up to 90 days within a 180-day period.
If you intend to work in Norway or remain for longer than 90 days, you must apply for a residence permit.
Visitor Visas and Residence Permits
Applications for a Visitor’s visa or residence permit from Pakistan and Afghanistan are handled by the visa hub in Abu Dhabi, and by the Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI) in Norway.
Further information is available on the following links:

Please note that the waiting times can be used as a guide, and may vary depending on the circumstances of the individual case.
Neither the embassy nor UDI can guarantee when a decision will be made.

Royal Norwegian Embassy, Abu Dhabi, does not respond to visa enquiries by telephone.
Their regional visa hub can be contacted at visa.abudhabi@mfa.no
Kindly note that they are unable to provide updates about the status of an application.
Also, due to a high number of enquiries, they do not reply to emails if the information is available on their website .
Applicants are therefore encouraged to consult the relevant websites before contacting the visa hub.
