Foreign nationals allowed to enter the Philippines without a visa for a maximum period of stay of thirty (30) days should meet all of the following requirements:
The applicant is travelling to the Philippines for business or tourism; Valid tickets for return to port of origin or next port of destination; Passport valid for at least six (6) months beyond the contemplated period of stay; and The applicant is not subject of deportation/blacklist order of the Department and the Bureau of Immigration.
