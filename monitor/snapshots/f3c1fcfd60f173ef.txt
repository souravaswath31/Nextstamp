Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA)
Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) Services
The Ministry of Interior and National Administration, State Department for Immigration and Citizen Services, Directorate of Immigration Services has developed and is operating the Republic of Kenya Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA).
All visitors including infants and children who intend to travel to the Republic of Kenya must have an approved Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) before the start of their journey.
Applicants shall visit the eTA website to make applications.
