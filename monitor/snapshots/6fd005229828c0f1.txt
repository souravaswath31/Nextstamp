Visa for Citizens of Certain Countries Resident Outside the UAE
This service enables the issuance of an entry visa for citizens of certain countries who are resident outside the UAE and hold permanent residence, a valid residence permit, or a valid visa for the United States, European Union Member States, the United Kingdom, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, or Canada, enabling them to enter the country in accordance with the applicable terms and conditions

Beneficiaries of this service are nationals of India, Kenya, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Philippines, and South Africa holding a valid visa or residence permit valid for a period of not less than six (6) months.
The beneficiary of this service must hold a residence permit issued by one of the following countries: United States of America, European Union Member States, United Kingdom, the Republic of Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, or Canada.
Indian nationals holding a valid US visa may benefit from this service in the same manner as those holding residence permits.
