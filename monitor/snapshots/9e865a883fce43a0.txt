Saltar a contenido principal Embajada del Perú en Singapur Visa - Frequently asked questions Guía 22 de marzo de 2026 Frequently asked questions: Do Singapore passport holders need a visa to go to Peru as tourists?
Singapore passport holders do not need a visa to go to Peru as tourists.
The passport must have a minimum of 6 months validity from the date of entry to Peru.
Singapore tourists can stay up to 90 days in Peru without a visa.
Do Singapore passport holders need a visa to go to Peru for business purposes?
Singapore passport holders who intend to travel to Peru for business purposes (for example signing contracts or engaging in remunerated activities) are not exempted of applying for a Business Visa.

When are Chinese and Indian passport holders exempted from applying to a Peruvian tourist or business visa?
Chinese and Indian passport holders do not need to apply for a tourist or business visa if they have any of the following visas: i) A visa from the United States, or Canada, or Australia, or the United Kingdom or any state that belongs to the Schengen Area.
(The visa must have minimum six-month validity) ii) A permanent residence in the United States, or Canada, or Australia, or the United Kingdom or any state that belongs to the Schengen Area.
The maximum number of days for staying in Peru under any of the above two conditions is 180 days, during a period of six months for Chinese passport holders, and one year for Indian passport holders.
