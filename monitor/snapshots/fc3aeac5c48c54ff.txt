THE LIST OF THE COUNTRIES TO BE EXEMPTED FROM TOURIST VISA REQUIREMENTS TO VISIT MONGOLIA UP TO 30 DAYS IN 2026
The Government of Mongolia has decided to exempt nationals of 34 countries from the 30-day visa requirements for tourism purposes in 2026.
THE LIST OF THE COUNTRIES TO BE EXEMPTED FROM TOURIST VISA REQUIREMENTS TO VISIT MONGOLIA UP TO 30 DAYS IN 2026
Hellenic Republic (Greece)
Republic of Lithuania
Principality of Liechtenstein
Grand Duchy of Luxembourg
Kingdom of the Netherlands
Principality of Monaco
United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland
Commonwealth of Australia
