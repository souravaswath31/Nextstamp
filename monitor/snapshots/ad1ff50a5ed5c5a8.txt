When planning a trip to Türkiye, we recommend that you contact the Turkish Embassy in Brasília or the Turkish Consulate General in São Paulo, in order to check the requirements for entry into the country (for example, minimum passport validity).
We remind you that, thanks to a bilateral agreement, Brazilians do not need a visa for tourist trips to Türkiye, with a maximum stay of 90 days.
