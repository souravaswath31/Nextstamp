Ministry of Foreign Affairs
Uganda High Commission in Abuja, Nigeria facilitates visa and entry services for all persons residing in Nigeria and accredited countries who intend to travel to Uganda.
All visa applications are processed online through the Uganda Electronic Visa Application System at https://visas.immigration.go.ug .
Applicants are encouraged to submit their applications and obtain approval prior to travel.
The High Commission provides guidance, verification, and assistance to applicants where necessary.
Travelers are advised to review all entry requirements and ensure compliance before making travel arrangements.
Uganda issues various categories of visas depending on the purpose of travel.
Ordinary/Tourist Visa – for tourism, family visits, or short stays.
Business Visa – for business trips, meetings, trade, or investment purposes.
Student Visa – for individuals undertaking studies in recognized Ugandan institutions.
Transit Visa – for travelers passing through Uganda to another destination.
East African Tourist Visa – a joint visa allowing travel between Uganda, Kenya, and Rwanda.
Visa Exempt Countries
Citizens of certain countries are exempt from visa requirements for short visits to Uganda.
A current list of visa-exempt countries is available on the e-Visa portal: https://visas.immigration.go.ug .
Possession of a visa does not automatically guarantee entry into Uganda.
Admission is determined by the immigration authorities at the point of entry.
All travelers must comply with the immigration laws and regulations of the Republic of Uganda.
