To whom the Visit Visa is applicable
The Visit Visa may be issued to the visitor that goes to Brazil for a stay of up to 90 days, with no intention of settling.
The engagement in paid work in Brazil is forbidden, except for per diem , lodging allowance, talent fee or managing partner remuneration.
All Nigerian citizens with regular passports are currently required to have a valid visa to enter Brazil.
Citizens of other nationalities may not be required to have a valid visa to enter Brazil.
Required documentation
Online Visa Request Form Receipt (RER) (hard copy);
International passport valid for at least six months (original);
visa fee payment slip (soft copy);
international passport Bio-Data page (soft copy);
previous travel history, if available, including from expired passports (soft copy):
previous visas, either Brazilian or other countries'; and
previous immigration stamps, either Brazilian or other countries'.
ICAO standard photograph (digital);
self-Introduction Letter (soft copy);
filled out manual visa application form  (soft copy);
Birth Certificate or Sworn Affidavit of Birth (soft copy);
if married, Marriage Certificate (soft copy);
flight reservation (soft copy);
itinerary and breakdown of the intended trip to Brazil (soft copy);
if being invited by a Brazilian institution or person:  a valid invitation letter  (soft copy); otherwise, a valid hotel reservation (soft copy);
introduction letter from Employer/Organization/ School (soft copy);
Certificate of Incorporation of the company (soft copy); and
complete Memorandum & Articles of Association (soft copy);
if the trip is self-sponsored:
salary slip (soft copy);
personal commercial bank statement (soft copy) for the last three months.
Microfinance bank statements are not acceptable
if the trip is sponsored by a third party:  additional sponsor's documents .
If minor under 18 years of age:
parental Authorization Form duly filled out and notarized either by the Cartório (Notary) in Brazil or by any of the approved Notaries in Nigeria (soft copy);
parental consent letter stamped at the Federal Ministry of Foreign Affairs (soft copy); and
birth Certificate (soft copy)
If going to Brazil for Business:
Company Bank Statement for the last three months (soft copy);
Certificate of Incorporation of the company (soft copy);
complete Memorandum &Articles of Association (soft copy);
Proof of Tax returns (soft copy); and
Invitation letter in Portuguese from the host company or organization (soft copy), which must include the CNPJ (Company registration details).
if applicable: Proof of previous transactions in Brazil (soft copy).
How to apply for a Visit Visa
pay the visa application fee;
start a new visa application in the Online Visa Request Form ( how to do it );
book your appointment for submission on:  https://ec-lagos.itamaraty.gov.br ;
wait for the validation of your application.
While waiting, monitor your email inbox.
You will receive the confirmation of the validation via email;
send your physical documents via courier with a return courier bag ( how to do it );
track the progress of the processing of your visa application ( how to do it ).
Once it has been processed, you will receive your physical documents via courier in the provided return envelope.
