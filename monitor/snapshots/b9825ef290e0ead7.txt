Visa & border crossings
Does the Jordan pass waive the visa fees?
Yes, Jordan pass waives the visa fees providing you will stay minimum of 2 whole nights (3 days) in Jordan.
Does Jordan Pass waive the visa fees of a multiple entry visa to Jordan?
No, Jordan pass waives the visa fees of only a single entry visa to Jordan.
How long is the single entry visa valid for?
The single entry visa is valid for 1 month, and can be extended easily from
any police station by paying extra fees.
Do I have to pay fees for visa extension if I hold a Jordan pass?
You can pay 3 JDs at the Airport for each day you stayed in Jordan after the 1 month validity, or you can pay 40 JDs at the Police Station for visa extension of 2 months.
How should I prove that I will stay 2 nights in Jordan?
You will not need to prove anything, you will get free entry, and when you are leaving the country the immigration system at the airport will check if you stayed more than 2 nights, if not, you will be directed to an office to pay the fees.

Which borders provide visa upon arrival?
Queen Alia Airport and Sheikh Hussein Bridge (Jordan River Border) provide visa upon arrival, Wadi Araba border (Eilat-Aqaba border) provides visa upon arrival only for Jordan pass holders who are eligible to get a visa upon arrival provided they stay a minimum of 2 whole nights in Jordan.
How will the visa fees be waived?
You need to present the Jordan pass in the visa office at the airport in order to waive the visa fees.
What are the fees for a single entry visa without the Jordan pass?
The fees for a single entry visa without the Jordan Pass are 40 JDs (about 60 USD).
What is the process for waiving the tourist visa with the purchase of Jordan pass for a resident in the gulf countries?
