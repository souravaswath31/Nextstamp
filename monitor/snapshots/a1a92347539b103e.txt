Visas and border measures
Each country or territory decides its own entry and exit rules, including health border screening measures.
To find out what you need to enter a destination, ask their embassy, consulate or immigration office.
Australian passport holders do not require a visa for stays up to 90 days if travelling for tourism, business meetings or conferences.
Other passport holders should check with the Singapore Immigration and Checkpoint Authority if they need a visa to enter Singapore.
At immigration, you may have to show:
a completed  Singapore Arrival Card (SGAC) (there's no charge for the SGAC).

a passport with at least 6 months validity
evidence of enough funds for your intended stay
a confirmed onward or return flight ticket
a valid visa to enter your next destination
