For citizens of Brazil
Citizens of Brazil do not need a short-stay visa to enter Slovenia.
For some countries, the exemption applies only to holders of biometric passports.
Please check the validity of the facilitation on the European Commission's website.
For full details of the procedure for obtaining a long-stay visa , please contact the relevant Slovenian diplomatic mission or consular post .
Detailed information on the conditions of entry and residence in the Republic of Slovenia can be found on the topic page Entry and residence.
