Visa regime Nationals of Philippines are required to obtain a visa to enter Montenegro.
Visas may be obtained from diplomatic and consular posts of Montenegro.
Diplomatic relations Altogether, relations between Montenegro and the Republic of Philippines may be observed as good and friendly The Republic of Philippines officially recognized Montenegro on the 26th of September 2009, and the diplomatic relations between the two countries were established on the same day.
