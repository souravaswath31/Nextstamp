eApplications Temporary residence in Serbia
You may enter Serbia with a visa from another country
This is an exception as otherwise you need a visa to enter Serbia.
Even though you are a national of a country whose citizens need a visa to enter and stay in the Republic of Serbia, we have great news for you!
Considering that you have one of the following documents - a valid Schengen visa, a UK visa, an EU member state visa, a USA visa, or you have a valid residence permit in a Schengen area country, an EU member state, or a US state, you may use the document to enter and stay in Serbia.
Keep in mind that on the basis of that visa/residence permit, you can stay in Serbia for a maximum of 90 days within a six-month period from the day you arrive, provided your documents remain valid for the entire length of stay.
If the validity of your visa or residence permit is shorter than the number of days you wish to stay in Serbia, you have the option to apply for a C visa, which will allow you to stay in Serbia for up to 90 days within a 180-day period.
In that case, read everything you need to know about C and apply electronically here .
Important A valid Schengen visa, UK visa, EU member state visa, USA visa, or a Schengen area country residence permit, EU member state residence permit or US state residence permit allow you to stay in Serbia for a short period, but do not allow you to regulate your long-term stay in Serbia.
If you want to stay in Serbia longer than the options listed above allow, you need to obtain a D visa.
For more information, return to My Entry / Visa / Residence Guide and select the "up to 36 months" stay option.
A few important notes for specific situations
If you have a Schengen, UK or USA visa and it is still valid, but it is in your old passport, no problem!
All you need to do is bring that old passport along with your new, valid passport.
In case you have a residence permit in the form of a special card instead of a sticker in your passport, all you have to do is show the card with a valid national passport when entering the Republic of Serbia.
When entering the Republic of Serbia, it is useful to know the rules that apply to all visitors, irrespective of whether they enter with or without a visa.
You can read all about the general entry requirements for Serbia on the page General Entry Requirements .

If, during your stay in the Republic of Serbia for up to 90 days, you intend to work as defined in employment contract or other contract proving the employment in accordance with labor regulations, the regulations for foreigners with granted temporary stay in Serbia on one of the grounds for issuing single temporary residence and work permit apply to you.
To get more information, return to the beginning of My Entry / Visa / Residence Guide and instead of "up to 90 days", choose the "up to 36 months" option for length of stay.
