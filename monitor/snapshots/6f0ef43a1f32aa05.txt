Korean Visa  Application  Guide Indian nationals must apply for  Korean Visa  and obtain it through the Visa Portal(www.visa.go.kr) & Visa Application Center(KVAC-VFS).
Please refer to the visa application guide as below.
Confirm Visa Type and Required Documents      https://www.visaforkorea-ce.com/visa-type.html 2.
Fill out and print the e-Form on the Visa Portal (Submit to the Visa Application Center after printing)        https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10204 3.
Visit the Visa Application Center(KVAC-VFS) for final submission      https://www.visaforkorea-ce.com/attend-centre.html 4.
Check application status on the Visa Portal      https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10301
