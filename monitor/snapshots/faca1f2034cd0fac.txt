Visa regime Nationals of Singapore may enter, pass through the territory of and stay in Montenegro up to 90 days, with a valid travel document, without a visa.
Diplomatic Relations Since the establishment of diplomatic relations, Montenegro and the Republic of Singapore have maintained friendly relations.
Montenegro is interested in further strengthening bilateral ties with Singapore, and the full valorization of potentials in areas of mutual importance.
Singapore recognized Montenegro on June 14, 2006, and diplomatic relations were established on June 30, 2006.
Diplomatic communication is carried out through the Permanent Missions of the two countries to the United Nations (in New York).
The two countries do not have a diplomatic presence at the ambassadorial level.
Political Contacts The Prime Minister of Montenegro, Milo Đukanović, paid an official visit to Singapore on May 5, 2009.
