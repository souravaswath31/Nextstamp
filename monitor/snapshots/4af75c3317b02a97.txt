Entry Requirements  This page provides the latest information on entry requirements for Korea.
If the information you require is not addressed on this page, please refer to  FAQ page (click) ,  (1) There is no requirement on the passport's remainder validity for entry.
You may travel to Korea as long as your passport remains valid throughout your stay in Korea.
(2) Foreigners entering Korea must have  either  K-ETA   OR   Visa ※ The Korean government has resumed several visa-free entry schemes for transfer passengers.
Please refer to the attachment for details.
※  For visa-free entry scheme for Jeju Island, please  click here .
K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) Visa﻿ Who should apply for K-ETA:  Nationals from countries that have visa-waiver or visa-free agreement with Korea and whose purpose of visit is tourism, business, discussions, conferences, short-term study with stay below 90 days...etc.
✔︎ Tip 1:  Check if you are eligible for K-ETA ○ Visit   K-ETA website (click)  and search for your country.
○ If you can find your country, it means you can apply for K-ETA instead of visa.
○ If you CANNOT find your country, it means you must apply for visa.
○ ✔︎ Tip 2:   The Korea government provides K-ETA exemption for certain nationalities (i.e Singapore) and age groups.
You may refer to the "Exceptions" section below.
How to apply:  Apply online at  www.k-eta.go.kr   (The Korean government did not designate any 3rd party agency or website for K-ETA applications)   When to apply:  You are advised to apply for K-ETA early as the Embassy has no control over the processing time.
Who to contact for assistance:   ﻿ You may contact the K-ETA Centre at   +82-2-2666-0463   or via the   online enquiry form (click) .
Note: K-ETA applications are not assessed or approved by the Embassy in Singapore.
Therefore, we would not be able to assist with checking application status or providing expedition for processing.
Who should apply for visa:  Nationals who are not eligible for K-ETA, whose K-ETA application is denied or whose purpose of visit is not covered under K-ETA.
How to apply:  All visa applications (except A-1 and A-2) must be submitted directly at  Korea Visa Application Center (click) , starting from 01 September 2026.
Advance reservation is required (no walk-ins).
Book a slot  here (click)   👈 Korea Visa Application Center (KVAC) 47 Scotts Road, #17-01 Goldbell Towers Singapore 228233 Contact: 6895-4700 /  kvac.support@visaforkorea-sg.com   Note:  If you have a valid multiple entry visa (C-3 type) in your old passport, you may use it to enter Korea  after  a  transfer of visa (click)   is successfully completed.
Please note that transfer applications are approved by the Embassy that issued your visa.
Therefore, the Embassy in Singapore can only approved transfer requests of visas issued in Singapore.
[Exceptions] The following groups of individuals do not require K-ETA or visa:  - Korean nationals  - APEC card holders with "KOR" listed on the card (excluding US and Canada)  - Registered foreigners (i.e holders of Registration Card)  - UN passport holders  - Transit passengers who will not clear the immigration in Korea (excluding Egypt, Sudan, Syria and Yemen)  -  (with effect from 09 Jan 2023)   Diplomatic and Official passport holders of K-ETA required countries  -  (with effect from 03 July 2023)   Nationals from K-ETA eligible countries aged 17 and below or aged 65 and above.
-  (Extended till 31 December 2026)   Countries exempted from K-ETA, including  Singapore.
Those who are subjected to exemption will get a pop-up message when they select their nationality during the application.
(3)  Submit an  e-Arrival card (click)   within 3 days before arrival in Korea (starting from 24 February 2025) (4) Singaporeans aged 17 and above are eligible to use  automated immigration clearance (click)  upon successful registration at Incheon Airport (starting from 01 December 2025) (5)  No  vaccination requirement and COVID-19 testing required.
