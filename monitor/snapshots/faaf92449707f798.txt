BUSINESS E-VISA FOR INDIAN CITIZENS 💼✈️
To strengthen economic relations and facilitate business exchanges between Brazil and India, the Government of Brazil has introduced a special category of electronic visa (e-visa) for Indian nationals travelling to Brazil for business purposes.
The business e-visa follows the same procedures as the in-person visa, but it eliminates the need to visit the Embassy.
The entire process is completed online: applicants upload all required documents, including a full copy of all pages of their passport, directly to the visa application form.
The request is then submitted to the Embassy of Brazil through the e-consular platform.
Once approved, the e-visa is issued in PDF format with a QR code and sent to the applicant’s registered email address.
Please note that the business e-visa application is examined and approved by the Embassy of Brazil and does not imply automatic issuance.
As the number of available e-visa slots is limited, applications are scheduled electronically and processed by the Embassy on the scheduled date.
📌 ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA – Business E-Visa
Indian citizens may apply for an electronic business visa ( business e-visa ) only if they meet ALL of the following requirements:
(i) 🕒 their stay in Brazil will not exceed 90 days; and
(ii) 💼 their travel purpose is strictly for BUSINESS activities, which are limited to: participation in meetings, fairs, and business events; prospecting commercial opportunities; signing contracts; conducting audits or consultancies; acting as crew member of aircraft or vessel; and the provision of technical assistance services or transfer of technology resulting from a contract, cooperation agreement, or arrangement concluded between a foreign legal entity and a Brazilian legal entity; and
(iiii) 🆔 applicants are fully able to electronically sign PDF documents using the DigiLocker e-signature function with their Aadhaar credentials.
⚠️ Visas for tourism, formal work (VITEM), study, family visits, or any other purposes must be applied for in person.
📝 APPLICATION PROCEDURE FOR THE BUSINESS E-VISA
🔍 Check your eligibility Make sure you fully meet the requirements listed above.
If in doubt, review them again or contact us at 📧 consular.newdelhi@itamaraty.gov.br
⚠️ Important: It is the applicant’s responsibility to choose the correct visa category.
Visa fees for the wrong category cannot be refunded.
📑 Double-check the documents required for a Business Visa (VIVIS) You may use our official checklist  🔗.
For an e-visa, besides the regular documents, a full copy of  all pages of your passport  is also required.
🖊️ Fill out the Visa Application Form
⚠️ IMPORTANT: 📤 At the last step of the Visa Application Form, you must upload all the required documents for a regular business visa, as well as a full copy of ALL PAGES of your passport , in good quality.
For your reference, the documents upload section of the Visa Application Form will look like this 👇
Documents upload section
📝 Save & E-SIGN your Form Receipt (RER) Once you have completed the steps above (eligibility check ✔️, documents ✔️, visa form ✔️), save the visa form receipt (RER) in PDF format and E-SIGN it through the DigiLocker app , using your Aadhaar credentials.
🚨 ATTENTION: After signing the form receipt, return to the documents upload section of the Visa Application Form and UPLOAD the e-signed RER receipt.
📤 Request your visa e-scheduling in the e-consular portal Finally, you may proceed to the e-consular portal to request your e-scheduling for the electronic visa.
For the e-visa, the e-scheduling is only a chronological reference for your visa processing.
👉 You will NOT be required to visit the Embassy at any stage of the procedure.
💳 Payment instructions for the visa fees will appear at the end of the e-consular form.
🚫 Visa fees are NOT refundable.
