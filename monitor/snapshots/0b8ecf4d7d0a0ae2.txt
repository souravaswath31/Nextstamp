Countries with a visa requirement and visa-free countries
Below you find a list of countries with a visa requirement and visa-free countries.
Countries with a visa requirement
If you are a citizen of one of the following countries, you must have a visa in order to enter Denmark:
Albania**** (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since May 2010.
As from 1 March 2012 non-biometric passports will no longer be valid for travel abroad)
Bosnia-Herzegovina**** (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since 15 October 2009)
Central African Republic
Democratic Republic of Congo
Georgia (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
This does not apply to holders of diplomatic and service/official passports or special passports.
All passports issued from 28 July 2014 are biometric passports)
Kosovo (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Kosovo has been issued biometric passports since October 2011)
Moldova**** (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since 1 January 2011)
Montenegro**** (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since May 2008.
All new passports are biometric)
North Macedonia (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since April 2007)
Sao Tomé and Principe
Serbia**** (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since July 2008)
Taiwan (Citizens with a passport issued by Taiwan which contains an identity card number are exempt from the visa requirement.
The same applies to citizens with old Taiwanese passports with "Republic of China" on the front page, if the stated place of birth is Taiwan and the passport contains an identity card number)
Türkiye*** (Turkish citizens who are to perform a service in Denmark are exempt from the visa requirement.
Ukraine**** (Citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since 12 January 2015)
Vanuatu ( Read more about the reintroduction of the visa requirement for citizens of Vanuatu )
Passports issued by the Palestinian Authority
If you are a citizen of one of the following countries, you do not need a visa in order to enter Denmark:
Albania **** (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passportshave been issued since May 2010)
Bosnia-Herzegovina**** (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since 15 October 2009)
Georgia (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
This does not apply to holders of diplomatic and service/official passports or special passports.
All passports issued from 28 July 2014 are biometric passports)
Kosovo (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Kosovo has been issued biometric passports since October 2011)
Moldova**** (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since 1 January 2011)
Montenegro**** (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement)
North Macedonia (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement)
Saint Kitts and Nevis
Serbia**** (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement)
Vincent and the Grenadines
Taiwan (Only citizens with a passport issued by Taiwan which contains an identity card number are exempt from the visa requirement.
The same applies to citizens with old Taiwanese passports with "Republic of China" on the front page, if the stated place of birth is Taiwan and the passport contains an identity card number)
Ukraine (Only citizens with biometric passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
Biometric passports have been issued since 12 January 2015)
* Citizens with diplomatic and service passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
** Citizens with diplomatic passports are exempt from the visa requirement, and citizens with a "Hong Kong Special Administrative Region" passport, a "Região Administrativa Especial de Macau" passport or a "British National Overseas" passport are exempt from the visa requirement.
*** Citizens with diplomatic, special and service passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
**** Citizens with diplomatic passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
***** Citizens with diplomatic and service passports and "official passports" are exempt from the visa requirement.
******  Citizens with biometric diplomatic and service passports are exempt from the visa requirement.
