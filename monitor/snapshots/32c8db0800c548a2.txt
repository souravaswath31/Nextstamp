Nationals Who Do Not Require Visa
Who doesn’t require visa?
Nationals of the countries listed below do not require visas to enter Zambia.
However, just like other non-citizen and non-residents arriving in Zambia by Air, they  must be in possession of valid return air tickets.
Those arriving by land and water must have enough funds for their stay in and departure from Zambia.
