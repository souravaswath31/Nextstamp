The unilateral visa exemption policy is effective from 15 March 2025 until 14 March 2028 .
** In accordance with Resolution No.11/NQ-CP of the Government dated 15 January 2025, under the Tourism Stimulus Program in 2025, citizens of Poland, Czech and Switzerland enjoy visa exemption with a stay duration of 45 days from the date of entry, regardless of passport type, on the basis of fufilling the following conditions:
(i) Entry conditions as prescribed by the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners in Viet Nam;
(ii) A passport that is valid for at least 06 months from the date of entry; and
(iii) Travel on tours organized by Viet Nam's inbound licensed tour operators, with a tour purchase confirmation.
The visa exemption policy under the Tourism Stimulus Program in 2025 is effective from 01 March 2025 until 31 December 2025 .
