VISAS FOR FOREIGN CITIZENS
Please read the following general information regarding the processing of visas at the Consulate General of Argentina in Sydney.
Exemption from visa requirement according to traveler's nationality.
The National Directorate of Migration maintains a list of nationalities and passport types in relation to visa requirements for travel to Argentina.
For more information, please visit the website on visas of the National Directorate of Migration - Visas .
Chinese and Indian citizens holding an ordinary passport with a valid U.S visa do not need a visa or AVE to enter Argentina for "tourism".
For citizens of the People's Republic of China, this applies to stays of up to 30 days.
For citizens of the Republic of India, this applies to stays of up to 90 days.
Accepted categories: B1/B2, B2, J, B1, O, P (P1, P2, P3), E y H-1B .
Citizens of the People's Republic of China who hold a B1, B1/B2 visa issued by the USA do not require a visa for business visits for stays of up to 30 days.
Those who do not fall within the provisions of the preceding paragraphs must apply for a visa.
***************************
As of January 15, 2026, permanent residence cards issued by the United States of America (“Green Cards”) shall be deemed valid and sufficient to authorize and facilitate entry into the National Territory for foreign nationals of the People’s Republic of China, the Dominican Republic, and the Republic of India, under the categories of “Tourist” and “Businesspersons”, without the need for a consular visa.
Australian citizens do not require a visa to enter Argentina as tourists for a stay of up to 90 days.
However, it is recommended that you purchase medical insurance prior to your trip that cover you during your stay in Argentina .
The Consulate General grants visas to foreign citizens who need them for the activities they are going to carry out in Argentina.
To know the visa requirements and to request an appointment for the mandatory visa interview, the applicant must send an email to the Visa Section of this Consulate General ( visas_csidn@mrecic.gob.ar ), specifying:
Full name and contact details (telephone and email).
If you will be using a passport from your country of origin.
Activity to be carried out in Argentina (tourism, work, study, etc.).
Tentative date of travel to Argentina and length of stay in Argentina.
The Consulate General conducts visa interviews by appointment only, on business days during regular business hours.
Please keep in mind the following recommendations:
Begin the visa process 3 months prior to the date of arrival in Argentina.
Submit your completed visa application at least 45 days in advance of your planned trip.
Do not make irrevocable travel plans until the visa has been granted.
It is advisable not to purchase tickets until the visa has been approved (the requirement involves submitting a flight reservation).
The visa application process is private and personal.
Applications on behalf of others are not accepted.
No one is informed about other people's applications.
There is no minimum remaining validity period of the passport required to enter Argentina, only that it is valid.
However, some flights to Argentina have stopovers in third countries, and airlines may require passports to have a minimum remaining validity period in accordance with the requirements of such countries.
Therefore, and in order to avoid inconveniences, it is suggested to consult the airline about the minimum remaining validity period of the passport required to travel.
When you pay a visa processing fee, you are not guaranteed that the visa will be granted.
The Consulate General evaluates each application and, if the visa is denied, the fee paid is not refunded to the applicant.
"Autorización de Viaje Electrónica" (AVE) - Electronic Travel Authorization
Some foreigners with a valid U.S.
visa category B2 / J / B1 / O / P (P1-P2-P3) / E / H-1B in their valid regular passport, and who require a visa to enter Argentina as a tourist, may choose between applying at the Consulate General for a tourist visa or processing and obtaining an AVE online.
For information on the AVE process, and nationalities covered, please visit the website of the National Directorate of Migration - AVE .
"Tramitación de Ingreso Electrónica (TIE 24H)" - Electronic Entry Processing
Foreigners who do not require a visa to enter Argentina as a tourist, but require a visa for other reasons (business, trade fairs and market studies, digital nomads, scientific, professional, technical, artistic, students, participants of exchange/volunteer programs, amateur athletes, scholarship holders) may choose between applying at the Consulate General for such a visa or applying and obtaining a TIE24H online.
For more information visit the TIE 24H web page of the National Directorate of Migration .
