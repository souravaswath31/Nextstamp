Special Notice to the Short Term Visitors to Sri Lanka
With effect from 25.05.2026, nationals of the following 40 countries are eligible to obtain tourist visa (ETA) free-of-charge for a period of 30 days.
UNITED SATATES OF AMERICA
Nationals of the above-mentioned countries holding Diplomatic, Official, Service, or Ordinary passports are eligible to obtain tourist visa (ETA)free-of-charge under this scheme.
Please note that all foreign nationals, including nationals of the above-mentioned 40 countries and nationals with the bilateral reciprocal agreements with Sri Lanka ( i.e.
Maldives, Seychelles and Singapore)  are required to obtain an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) prior to their arrival in Sri Lanka.
As per the bilateral reciprocal agreement between Sri Lanka and Maldives, Maldivian nationals will receive 90 days tourist visa through ETA system.
The Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) for nationals of the above-mentioned 40 countries and for nationals of Maldives, Seychelles and Singapore will be processed free of charge.
How ever, ETA fee paid by the applicants prior to 25.05.2026 are non-refundable
Under this scheme, tourists are entitled to a tourist visa (ETA) free of charge valid for 30 days (Maldivian nationals will receive 90 days), with double-entry facility permitted from the date of first arrival in Sri Lanka within the 30-day validity period.
Applicants who have obtained a free visa and wish to remain in Sri Lanka beyond 30 days may apply for a visa extension upon payment of the applicable visa fee.
Please note that except the national eligible to apply ETA free of Charge mentioned above, nationals of all other countries shall continue to be subject to the general rules and regulations governing the Sri Lanka ETA system.
Categories Exempted from the ETA Requirement
An Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) is not required for passengers travelling to Sri Lanka who hold any of the following valid documents or authorizations:
A valid Sri Lankan Resident Visa.
A valid Sri Lankan Permanent Resident Visa.
A valid Sri Lankan Dual Citizenship Certificate, together with the relevant endorsement in the foreign passport.
A valid Registration Certificate issued under Section 5(2) of the Citizenship Act of Sri Lanka, applicable to passengers below 22 years of age, together with the endorsement in their foreign passport.
A valid Entry Visa issued by the Department of Immigration and Emigration of Sri Lanka.
A valid Landing Endorsement issued by the Department of Immigration and Emigration of Sri Lanka.
Any other valid travel authorization or exemption recognized under the Immigrants and Emigrants Act of Sri Lanka, including persons travelling to Sri Lanka for official or diplomatic purposes who are exempt from the ETA requirement, as specified in Annex 1 .
