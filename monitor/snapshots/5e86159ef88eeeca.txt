Visa Information for Australian Passport Holders, 19.05.2026
VISA FOR TÜRKİYE (FOR AUSTRALIAN CITIZENS) Holders of ordinary Australian passports are exempt from visa requirements for touristic
visits and transit travel for a period of 90 days within any 180-day period.
Holders of official passports are required to obtain a visa.
kindly advised to have a passport valid for at least 6 months as from the date of your arrival in Türkiye.
Official Australian passport holders (diplomatic,
service, special and official passports) need to submit pre-visa application on
the web site www.visa.gov.tr to obtain their sticker-visa.
Following the pre-visa application and booking the
appointment, the applicants should apply to our Consulate General in person to lodge
their visa request by submitting their visa application forms duly signed and
their passports as well as the original copies of the supporting documents that
have already been uploaded on the pre-visa application system.
All information in relation to obtaining Turkish visas can be accessed from the
web sites of www.konsolosluk.gov.tr and www.visa.gov.tr (PLEASE BEWARE/CAUTIOUS OF VISA FRAUD/SCAMS VIA
UNAUTHORIZED WEBSITES AND DO NOT APPLY THROUGH ANY WEBSITE OTHER THAN THE
OFFICIAL TURKISH GOVERNMENT WEBSITES OTHERWISE YOU CAN BE A VICTIM OF FRAUD) P.S.
An information note concerning the visa
types granted by Türkiye is available in the following address: http://www.mfa.gov.tr/visa-information-for-foreigners.en.mfa
