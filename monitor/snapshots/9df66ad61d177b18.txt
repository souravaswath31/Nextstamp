Notice on Extension of K-ETA Temporary Exemption (until December 31, 2026)
The Ministry of Justice decided to extend the temporary exemption period for one more year to stimulate the tourism industry, the exemption will be effective until December 31, 2026 (KST).
*Extended K-ETA Temporary Exemption Period :  From January 1, 2026 (Thu) to December 31, 2026 (Thu) (KST) The countries/regions that are currently exempt from K-ETA  (including the U.S.)  are subject to this extension.
Those who are subject to the exemption will get a pop-up message when they select their nationality during the application.
However, travelers who wish to receive benefits from K-ETA approval, such as not having to submit an arrival card, may apply for K-ETA; in that case, the application fee will be charged.
※ Those who have obtained K-ETA can use it until its expiration date and the application fee paid is non-refundable.
