30-Day Visa Fee Waiver (AU, Commonwealth & La Francophonie Members)
Citizens of countries that are members of the African Union, Commonwealth and La Francophonie.
Albania, Algeria, Andorra, Antigua and Barbuda, Armenia, Australia, Bangladesh, Barbados, Belize, Belgium, Botswana, Brunei Darussalam, Burkina Faso, Bulgaria, Cameroon, Canada, Cape Verde, Cambodia, Comoros, Congo, Cyprus, Dominica, Djibouti, Egypt, Eswatini, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Fiji, France, Gabon, Grenada, Greece, Guinea-Bissau, Guyana, India, Kiribati, Jamaica, Laos, Lebanon, Lesotho, Liberia, Libya, Luxembourg, Macedonia, Madagascar, Malawi, Mali, Malaysia, Malta, Mauritania, Maldives, Monaco, Morocco, Mozambique, Namibia, Nauru, New Zealand, Niger, Nigeria, Pakistan, Papua New Guinea, Romania, Saharawi republic, Somalia, South Africa, Sudan, Switzerland, Togo, Tunisia, The Bahamas, The Gambia, Tonga, Tuvalu, Trinidad and Tobago, Saint Lucia, St Vincent and the Grenadines, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Sri Lanka, United Kingdom, Vietnam, Vanuatu, Zambia and Zimbabwe are granted visa on arrival , waived visa fees , and 30 days stay .
90-Day Visa Free for Ordinary Passport Holders
Citizens of the following countries receive a 90-day visa free of charge : Angola, Benin, Central African Republic, Chad, Côte d’Ivoire, Democratic Republic of Congo, The Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis, Ghana, Guinea, Indonesia, Haiti, Mauritius, Philippines, Senegal, Seychelles, Sierra Leone, Sao Tome and Principe, Singapore, State of Qatar .
90-Day Visa Free for Diplomatic & Service/Official Passports
The following countries have visa waiver agreements for holders of Diplomatic or Service/Official Passports, valid for 90 days : Angola, Benin, People’s Republic of China (including Public Affairs passport), Côte d’Ivoire, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of Congo, Djibouti, Ethiopia, The Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis, Gabon, Ghana, Guinea, Indonesia, India, Israel, Haiti, Mauritius, Mozambique, Morocco, Namibia, Philippines, Sao Tome and Principe, Senegal, Seychelles, Singapore, South Africa, Turkey, United Arab Emirates .
Applying for a Visa Through the Rwandan High Commission
Applicants may submit visa applications in person or by post.
Postal applications must include a self-addressed recorded/special delivery envelope.

Visa Exemption – Additional Information
Rwandans with Dual Nationality May use their Rwandan national ID to enter Exempt from visa fees even if travelling with foreign passports Foreign Residents in Rwanda May enter using their resident ID card Can use electronic gates at Kigali International Airport
Children Under 16 Children under 16 travelling with parents who hold foreign passports, and a Rwandan ID are exempt from visa fees.
East African Community (EAC) Citizens
Citizens of Burundi, Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and South Sudan : Receive a 6-month renewable pass No fee
Visa on Arrival (General)
Effective 1 January 2018, citizens of all countries may receive a 30-day visa on arrival without prior application.
Citizens of non–fee-exempt countries must pay the standard visa fee: $50 USD
Visa Extensions & Change of Status Some visa classes may be extended Must apply before visa expiry 5-day grace period after expiry (no penalty) After 5 days, penalties may apply Some visas allow change of status inside Rwanda (except group tourist visa)
Payment for Visa Application
The High Commission does NOT accept cash, cheques, or postal orders.
Payments must be made via bank transfer, online transfer, or direct bank deposit.
